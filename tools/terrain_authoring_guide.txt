Flusion Ecumenopolis v19 terrain.png authoring guide
==================================================

terrain.png is the PRIMARY world-definition file.

WATER / GEOGRAPHY
  255,255,255  PURE WHITE   = OCEAN / ordinary sea
  0,0,200                    = INLAND SEA (navigable)
  0,255,255    PURE CYAN    = LAKE

IMPASSABLE EXCEPTION COLORS
  255,255,0    PURE YELLOW  = IMPASSABLE DESERT
  0,0,0        PURE BLACK   = IMPASSABLE RELIC
  128,128,128  HALF WHITE   = IMPASSABLE GLACIER
  255,0,0      PURE RED     = IMPASSABLE MOUNTAIN
  0,255,0      PURE GREEN   = IMPASSABLE JUNGLE
  0,0,255      PURE BLUE    = IMPASSABLE OCEAN

Everything else is LAND and uses the normal terrain RGB/alias table.

Actual pure cyan is 0,255,255.
255,255,0 is pure yellow and is reserved for IMPASSABLE DESERT.

OPTIONAL EXTRA INPUTS
  impassable.png            White background; pure green ADDS impassable land.
  below_sea_level_mask.png  White normal, blue water reference, red drained seabed.
  boundary.png              Optional province-shape constraints.
  rivers.bmp                Optional; missing file generates blank indexed rivers.bmp.

base_map.png is obsolete and ignored.

Minimum theoretical input:
  input/terrain.png
